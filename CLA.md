## 1. 定义 / Definitions

1.1 “**贡献**”指你有意提交给项目方、用于纳入或修改本项目的任何作品(代码、文档、翻译、设计、图标、数据等),包括通过 Pull Request、补丁、Issue 附件、邮件或其它方式提交的内容,且未被你书面明确标注为“非贡献”。
"**Contribution**" means any work of authorship that You intentionally submit to Us for inclusion in or modification of the Project (code, documentation, translations, designs, icons, data, etc.), via pull request, patch, issue attachment, email or otherwise, and that You have not conspicuously marked in writing as "Not a Contribution".

1.2 “**项目**”指字录（SubRec）及项目方维护的相关软件与文档。 / "**Project**" means SubRec (字录) and related software and documentation maintained by Us.

1.3 “**商业版本**”指项目方(或其被许可人)以非 GPL 条款(包括闭源、专有或商业许可)发布或销售的项目版本、衍生版本或产品。
"**Commercial Versions**" means any version, derivative or product of the Project that We (or our licensees) distribute or sell under terms other than the GPL, including proprietary or commercial licenses.
