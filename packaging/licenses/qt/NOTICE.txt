Qt Multimedia FFmpeg
--------------------

windeployqt can deploy Qt Multimedia's FFmpeg backend (multimedia/ffmpegmediaplugin.dll) with
the FFmpeg DLLs built by The Qt Company (avcodec-61.dll, avformat-61.dll, avutil-59.dll,
swresample-5.dll, swscale-8.dll; an LGPL build, version recorded in
sbom/qtmultimedia-6.8.0.spdx). They are separate from SubRec's own FFmpeg (avcodec-62.dll
and the other six DLLs listed in sources/BUILD_CONFIG.txt; see licenses/ffmpeg/ and sources/).
FFmpeg release sources: https://ffmpeg.org/releases/

Qt 自带的 FFmpeg（avcodec-61 等，LGPL 构建）与 SubRec 自己的 FFmpeg（avcodec-62 等）是两套独立的库。
